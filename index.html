I am a machine learning researcher at Columbia University, where I am advised by Micah Goldblum and Adam Block. I also conduct course research with John Hewitt. My research interests lie in the mathematical foundations of machine learning: using tools from statistics, optimization, and learning theory to understand why large models work, and to build AI systems that are more capable and more reliable.

Before that, I was an Assistant Vice President at Barclays Capital, where I led research on AI and machine learning for managing multi-billion-dollar portfolios. I also have spent time in Hong Kong before, where I was advised by Gavin Feng and worked on machine learning methods for empirical asset pricing.

My inspiration is Oppenheimer. I came to the United States to study where the field is being made and, as any learning theorist would, to minimise regret. I am dedicated to research on AI that helps shape the world into a better place for humanity.

Outside research, I play water polo and badminton, and I like dogs and cats.
